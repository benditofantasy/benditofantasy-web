Hi, friends.

We are about to kick off another tournament, and there is no better place to start than by thanking and welcoming everyone who is back for another season with Bendito Fantasy. This will already be our **ninth season**, and as always, we continue to evolve, grow, and look for new ways to interact with the huge community that has formed around this great game we know as Fantasy Premier League.

Today, it is time to talk about one of our traditions: **the Copa Santamaria**.

## Twenty spots. Not one more.

The Copa Santamaria is the cup competition we organize every year at Bendito Fantasy, named in honor of Julio Santamaria, one of our longest-standing Patreons and someone who has been part of Bendito Fantasy for many years. The time has come to kick things off once again and start handing out the tickets for a new edition.

The Cup is limited to only **20 participants**, keeping the same size and format as a Premier League season. Every Gameweek, two teams face each other head-to-head, winning or losing based on the points they score that week. The big difference compared with the general Bendito Fantasy mini-league is that simply signing up is not enough.

**You have to earn one of those 20 spots.**

Traditionally, the way to do that has been through our trivia challenges: one question, one correct answer, and one ticket to the Copa Santamaria. Over time, the search for those 20 participants has become a Bendito Fantasy tradition of its own, and this year it is back, although with a small change to the way we play.

## Now it is your turn to ask

We will still have trivia challenges, but this season we are also turning the dynamic over to the community. In some of these challenges, Bendito Fantasy will announce a theme, and those who want to earn one of the tickets to the Copa Santamaria will have to do something different: **come up with the question**.

Users will be able to post their proposed questions related to the announced theme using **#CopaSantamaria** and tagging **@benditofantasy**. Bendito Fantasy will review the submissions and select one of them. Posting a question does not mean the challenge has started; only when Bendito announces the selected question will the race to find the answer officially begin.

For a question to be considered, whoever submits it must also send us the correct answer via direct message. Obviously, the answer should not be posted publicly alongside the question: we need to know it so we can confirm that the trivia has a valid solution, verify that the proposed answer is correct, and later identify the winner.

Once the trivia is officially active, whoever finds the solution must post their answer while tagging **@benditofantasy**, allowing us to identify who answered correctly first. If the answer is correct, both the person who created the selected question and the person who solved it will earn their spot in this year's Copa Santamaria.

> **Knowing the answer still comes with a reward. But now, knowing how to ask the question does too.**

## What are we looking for?

We want clever, verifiable questions related to Fantasy, football, the Premier League, and the subjects surrounding them. We are not necessarily looking for impossible questions, but rather challenges that reward Fantasy knowledge, research, data, observation, and, above all, creativity.

> **We are not looking for impossible questions, but challenges that reward knowledge, research, data, observation, and creativity.**

Questions that find interesting ways to incorporate the tools available at Bendito Fantasy will also receive priority. We want you to explore, search, and discover what you can find within our own ecosystem, especially when that information can be used to create a question that cannot simply be solved with a quick search.

Bendito Fantasy reserves the right to accept, reject, or simply not select any of the questions submitted. Not every proposal will necessarily become an official trivia challenge, and Bendito will determine which question opens each challenge.

So get creative.

## First category: FPL History

And there is no reason to wait any longer. The first category kicking off the trivia challenges for the **2026/27 Copa Santamaria** is:

**FPL History: records, players, and curiosities.**

It is time to put those first questions together. Remember to use **#CopaSantamaria**, tag **@benditofantasy**, and make sure you are subscribed to our YouTube channel.

We will take it from there.

There are only 20 spots.

**The search starts now.**
